The AI Exposure Card

Six questions. Check the ones that are true. Each one has a name, and a cost: pipeline, revenue, or time.

This isn't about whether you use AI. Everyone does now. It's about which decisions you've quietly stopped making yourself, and what that's already costing your GTM motion.

  • 01
    You built or updated your ICP using AI's suggestion, without pulling your own closed won and churn data first.
  • 02
    You approved or recommended a discount based on an AI generated framework, without checking your actual margin on that deal.
  • 03
    You have an automation running right now that you built without knowing how many times a month the process actually runs.
  • 04
    You picked a tool by comparing AI generated pros and cons, without auditing what you already pay for that overlaps.
  • 05
    There's a number you report to leadership regularly that you have not personally verified against raw data in the last 30 days.
  • 06
    If your boss asked why a specific number in your last report is what it is, your honest answer would include the words "the tool."
