
Pull This Before You Decide

A working list. For each decision, the data you have to get yourself first, because no model has it.

Before you define or change your ICP
Last fifty closed won accounts. Last fifty churned. Win rate and retention broken out by segment, size, and source. Sales cycle length by segment. Look for what your best accounts share that your worst ones do not.

Before you approve a discount
Current margin on this deal. What this customer has been given before. What comparable accounts are paying. Whether anyone else in their industry will hear about it.

Before you automate a process
How many times it runs per quarter. Minutes it takes per run. Who currently owns it. Whether the real bottleneck is actually upstream of the step you are automating.

Before you buy a tool
What you already own that overlaps. Seat utilization on your current stack. What your data looks like going in. Who on the team will actually use it daily.

Before you trust a reported number twice
When the underlying tracking was last verified. A raw sample of twenty records checked by hand against the report. Whether anything upstream changed since the report was built.

Before you present anything to leadership
Your top five deals reviewed individually, outside the model. Confirmation each champion is still there. Your own answer to why, phrased without naming a tool.

The rule
If you cannot produce the data behind a decision, you are not making the decision. You are repeating one.
