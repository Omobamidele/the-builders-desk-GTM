## Now write the revenue version

Use this formula: **[what you built] → [what metric moved] → [the dollar or time value]**

Example from the lesson:
> Tech version: "I built an automation that enriches leads and routes them by score."
> Revenue version: "This cut lead response time from 2 days to 20 minutes — the single biggest lever on conversion — worth an estimated $X in recovered pipeline per quarter."
