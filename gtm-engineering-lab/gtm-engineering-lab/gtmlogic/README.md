## The core idea (short version)

Every major GTM framework — Gartner's, Bain's, GTM Partners' operating system — converges on the same four things, regardless of what the job title is called this year:

1. Who you sell to
2. Why they'd buy
3. How you reach them
4. Whether the economics work

The title changes. That doesn't.
