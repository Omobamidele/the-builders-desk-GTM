# 58 free GTM courses and certifications in one sheet

For you if you're in sales, RevOps, lead gen or business development, or you're moving into a GTM role and want to learn without paying for it.

Builder's Desk · Week 15 · by Mo Bamidele, GTM Engineer

## What this is
58 courses and certifications from HubSpot, Clay, Apollo, Salesforce Trailhead, LinkedIn, n8n, Zapier, Make, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Kaggle, Microsoft and more. Every one is free to take. For each one you get who it's for, how long it takes, whether you get a certificate or badge, what (if anything) costs money around it, and one line on why it helps.

## How it was checked (read this first)
- Checked on Nov 19, 2026. Every link loaded on that date
- Only courses that are free to take are in the sheet. If a course needed payment, it was left out
- Some courses are free but teach a paid tool (for example Sales Navigator, Instantly or Pipedrive). The Cost notes column says so, so you can decide if it's worth your time
- Times are copied from the course page. If the page didn't give a total time, it says "Not stated"
- Level comes from the course page where one is given. Where it isn't, the level is my guide, not the provider's
- "Certificate?" means: **Yes free** (a free certificate of completion or certification), **Badge** (a free digital badge or Trailhead badge), **No** (no certificate mentioned), **Not stated** (the page doesn't say)
- Courses change and get retired. If a link stops working, search the course name on the provider's site

## What's inside
- `free-gtm-courses.xlsx`: the full sheet, with filters on every column
- `free-gtm-courses.csv`: the same sheet as a CSV for Google Sheets, Notion or Airtable

Columns: Course / certification, Provider, Category, Best for role(s), Level, Time to complete (as stated), Certificate?, Cost notes, Link, Why it helps.

Categories: CRM (7), Outbound & prospecting (11), Data & enrichment (4), Automation (7), RevOps & analytics (8), Sales skills (3), AI for GTM (15), Marketing (3).

## How to use it
1. Open the sheet and filter **Best for role(s)** to the role you have or want
2. Pick one course you can finish this week. Short beats ambitious
3. Build something small with what you learned, and write down what it does. A badge says you watched it. A build shows you can do it
4. Add the certificate or badge to your LinkedIn, then move to the next one
