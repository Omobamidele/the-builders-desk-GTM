# 4-week plan: from sales to a first GTM build

One small thing per week. About 3 to 4 hours a week. Use made-up or public data only, never your company's customer data.

## Week 1: Get clear on the lead flow (CRM)
- Write down how a lead moves in your team: where it comes from, who owns it, what changes it
- Draw it as a simple list of steps. Mark where it can go wrong
- Output: one page you could show in an interview

## Week 2: Build that flow with 10 made-up leads
- Use Clay, n8n or Zapier (start with whichever you can get access to, then try the others)
- Build: take 10 made-up leads, add the missing details, give each a score with a reason, send each to an owner
- Include at least 2 rows with missing data and decide what should happen to them

## Week 3: Add the checks
- Learn what an API request is by sending one simple request
- Learn one SQL SELECT with a WHERE
- Add a check to your build: a row that fails goes to a "needs a person" list instead of disappearing

## Week 4: Write it up
- One page: the problem, how it works, how it can fail, the result of your 10-lead test
- Put it in a public place you control and link it on your profile
- Pick 5 postings for the role you want and check your page against what they ask for

## When you apply
- Read the requirements section. Counts in this kit show what is common, not what is required for the job you want
- Use the words from the posting that you can honestly back up with your build
